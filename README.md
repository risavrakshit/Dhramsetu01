My project is called DharmSetu, an AI-powered Lawyer Management and Recommendation System.

The main objective of this project is to help people understand their legal concerns and find suitable lawyers more easily. Sometimes, people do not know which category of lawyer they need for their particular problem. DharmSetu aims to make this process simpler using AI.

The application has two main sections: the Lawyer Section and the Customer Section.

In the Lawyer Section, lawyers can create their professional profiles by providing details such as their name, specialization, years of experience, contact information and educational background. They can also manage their own profiles.

In the Customer Section, users can describe their legal problems. The Case Insight Engine, powered by the Groq API, is designed to analyze the problem, identify the relevant legal category, assess the complexity, provide general guidance and recommend suitable lawyers from the available profiles.

Another feature is The Counsel Corner, an AI-powered emotional-support chatbot designed to respond empathetically and help users manage stressful situations.

For the technology stack, I used React and TypeScript for the frontend, Supabase and PostgreSQL for database management, Google OAuth for authentication, and the Groq API for AI integration.

One important aspect of the project is access control. The intended design allows lawyers to manage their own profiles while customers can view available lawyers without modifying their information.

I developed this as an individual project, which helped me understand how different components of a full-stack application work together, including frontend development, database integration, authentication and AI integration.

Overall, my goal with DharmSetu is to make the initial process of finding appropriate legal assistance more accessible while keeping AI-generated information within appropriate limitations.
